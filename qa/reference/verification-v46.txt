Remaining limits
Extreme rapid crumpling can still stutter and self-intersect, as it did in v4.5.
Stress crossings by checkpoint: plush [0,4,16]; short pile [122,178,264], identical
before and after. This release improves speed; it does not claim arbitrary folds are
intersection-free. Standard stacking regression remains clean.
